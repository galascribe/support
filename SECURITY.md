# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't** report security vulnerabilities in a public issue.

Email [support@galascribe.com](mailto:support@galascribe.com) with the subject line
**"Security report"** and include:

- what you found and where (the affected address, page or feature)
- the steps to reproduce it
- the impact you believe it has

We'll acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we investigate, and let you know when it's
fixed. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly, and
don't access, change or delete data that isn't yours while testing.

## Scope

- The Galascribe App at app.galascribe.com, and its API
- Sites published with Galascribe
- Galascribe's open-source packages in [rathnasgala2](https://github.com/rathnasgala2) and on npm
  under `@rathnasgala2`
